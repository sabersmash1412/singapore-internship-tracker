## Collection and quality controls

- Investigated hundreds of public board URLs and admitted only feeds with verified Singapore coverage and complete responses. Unverified slugs, generic empty boards and sources requiring unsupported collectors were excluded.
- Added the Workable public published-job collector with full descriptions, publication dates and structured internship/location fields. Identical shortcode records from different locations become one listing; conflicting content fails completeness. Missing detail text and invalid envelopes protect prior history. The shared Workable host receives paced requests and rate-limit cooldowns.
- Reviewed all newly matched titles and descriptions for ambiguous roles, including Razer's Product Developer, QCP's trading/quantitative work, EPOS's CRM automation, Disney's security and Sanofi's quality systems. Corporate Global Security Operations and Quality System Management are excluded without an explicit IT/cybersecurity/software qualifier. Data analytics, quant trading and business-process automation remain in scope.
- Most new employer boards currently have no matching internships. Coverage does not imply a verified internship programme or formal SME-size status.
- Staffing agencies and unnamed portfolio-company roles were excluded from this batch to avoid attributing client vacancies to the wrong employer. Some parent-company career boards include subsidiaries under the parent label.
- Listings use employer-hosted application links. Unknown values remain `-`; consecutive company names retain the existing arrow formatting. Titles and explicit employer metadata drive classification; generic engineering/research roles may still be missed.
- The external cron schedule and failure notifications remain unchanged. Future endpoint availability and GitHub runner connectivity can differ from this local collection.
