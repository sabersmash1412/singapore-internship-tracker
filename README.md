## How to read this list

- Every role has Singapore location evidence and technical-role evidence. Internship status comes from the title or the employer’s structured employment type. Generic APAC/remote listings are excluded.
- **Period** quotes what the employer states when extractable. “Not stated” is not a guess about summer or a university semester.
- **⚠️ Older period** flags a stated period entirely in a previous calendar year, even though the employer still lists the role. Multiple stated periods are preserved; confirm conflicting dates with the employer.
- **Employer posted** and **First seen** are different dates. Missing publication dates remain unknown.
- Missing jobs leave the open table after two complete checks; a returned job explicitly outside our scope is removed immediately. Failed checks cannot close roles.
- “Open” means listed in the employer feed. Confirm availability, dates, eligibility and allowance with the employer. Older start windows may remain advertised.
